# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
A mixed audience with no single primary visitor:
- People who want to try the apps Dawn Studio ships (realufo.org, MoneyRate, Holdr, ClipBin, Pennyvest, Watch the Night, Global News Shorts, openmusic, OpenQ).
- Builders and developers following the build-in-public story, the blog, and the open-source code.
- Prospective collaborators who want to see what Hector has made before reaching out.

## Product Purpose
hectorchan.com is the personal site and blog of Hector Chan, an independent creator and founder of Dawn Studio, a one-person software studio. It is the hub that connects Hector, the studio, and every app the studio ships.

Success means:
- Visitors click through to the apps (realufo.org is the flagship).
- Visitors read the blog and follow the build story.
- Visitors come away remembering Hector as an independent creator and the person behind Dawn Studio.

Email contact (hello@hectorchan.com) stays available but is not a primary success measure.

## Positioning
One person who builds things they use, ships them fast, and makes everything public and free: the code is open, the sites cost nothing, and there are no dark patterns. "Honest is my weakness — and my edge." The work is real and live, shown with real numbers from real repositories.

## Operating Context
- Static personal site: home (studio, apps, hackathon story, about, blog, contact), blog index and posts, privacy page, 404, and an unlinked private console at `/admin/`.
- Blog posts are first-person build stories (RealUFO 7-day rebuild with Claude, openmusic, Muse AI review).
- Installable as a PWA (manifest + service worker).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Plain static HTML/CSS/JS with no build step.
- Deployed on Cloudflare.
- No tracking cookies; analytics is Cloudflare Web Analytics only (cookieless). No accounts, sign-up, comments, or forms.
- All work is open source and free to use; that claim must stay true wherever it appears.
- Languages: English, with Traditional Chinese (繁中) support where relevant.
- `/admin/` is a private shortcuts page, not linked anywhere and not part of the public experience.

## Brand Commitments
- Names: Hector Chan (personal), Dawn Studio (company behind all apps, dawnstudio.app).
- Voice: first-person, plain, honest, a little self-deprecating; short sentences; no hype.
- Assets: `assets/avatar.png`, `assets/dawn-studio-logo.png` (official logo from dawnstudio.app), `assets/dawn-studio-mark.webp`, app icons.

## Evidence on Hand
- Live apps with real URLs and GitHub repos (listed on the home page).
- realufo.org facts: 564 records across 9 archives; hackathon commit history (265 / 327 / 117 commits on Oct 2–4, 2026).
- Three published blog posts under `blog/`.
- No testimonials, press, user counts, or revenue figures exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Show the shipped work first; the apps are the proof.
2. Honest over impressive: real numbers, real links, no inflated claims.
3. Open and free is a fact, not a slogan. Keep it verifiable via GitHub links.
4. Stay light: static, fast, no tracking, nothing a visitor has to sign up for.
5. One person's voice throughout; it should read like Hector wrote it.
