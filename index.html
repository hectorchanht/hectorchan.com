The story

The RealUFO hackathon

I paused the project for two months, not knowing what it could become. Then a stranger posted in the discussion board — and I never stopped building. What followed was a 7-day sprint with Claude as my pair.

Jul – Sep

The archive exists but sits quiet. I’m not sure what it wants to be.

Early Oct

A stranger posts in /board/uap. Someone out there cares. The spark.

Oct 2 – 4

The sprint: 265 commits, then 327, then 117. Search, citations, boards, RAG Q&A, release tracker — built live, deployed daily.

Now

realufo.org is live, indexed, and growing. I handed its ongoing care to Muse — and kept building.
